Privacy Policy for Biosweep Connect – Tools

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Biosweep Connect – Tools ("the App") is operated by Biosweep Canada Corporation ("Biosweep Canada"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, retained, and protected when employees and authorized users use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email addresses and account identifiers used for authentication.
  • Receipt images and extracted receipt information, including vendor, date, total, and taxes.
  • Scoping information, including job notes and measurements.
  • Treatment reports and related business information entered into the App.
  • Calendar event details, including titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, and attendee email addresses.
  • Information identifying which authorized employee created an event through the App.
  • Event reminder preferences and information needed to prepare daily calendar briefings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate authorized employees and provide access to the App.
  • Process receipts and prepare business reports.
  • Calculate and document scoping measurements.
  • Prepare treatment reports and related business documentation.
  • Send receipts, scoping information, and treatment reports by email.
  • Create and manage authorized events on the Biosweep Canada Google Calendar.
  • Invite event attendees and configure event reminders.
  • Send employees personalized daily emails about events relevant to them.
  • Provide designated administrators with a daily summary of all events on the Biosweep Canada calendar.

3. Google Calendar Integration

The App integrates with Google Calendar to support business scheduling. With authorization from the calendar account owner, Biosweep Canada may access event information on the designated Biosweep Canada calendar, including events created directly through Google Calendar.

Calendar information is used for business scheduling, attendee invitations, event reminders, and daily schedule briefings. Employees using the App may create events and may modify or delete only the events they are authorized to manage through the App. Designated administrators may receive information about all events on the company calendar.

Google Calendar information is not used for advertising, sold to third parties, or used to develop or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Access to Google Calendar information is limited to the business functions described in this policy.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Calendar Invitations and Daily Briefings

Calendar invitations may be sent to the email addresses of attendees selected for an event. Event information may be included in Google Calendar notifications and invitations.

Authorized employees may receive a daily email summarizing events in which they are involved. Designated Biosweep Canada administrators may receive a daily email summarizing all events scheduled on the company calendar.

These emails may contain event titles, times, locations, descriptions, and other scheduling details relevant to their recipients.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

The App uses Firebase and related Google Cloud services for authentication and supporting application functionality. Google Calendar processes calendar information used for company scheduling.

Authorized email delivery services may process recipient addresses and message contents as needed to send business reports, invitations, and daily calendar briefings.

6. Information Sharing

Biosweep Canada does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized employees, designated administrators, event attendees, and service providers when necessary for the business functions described in this policy, or when required by law.

7. Data Security

Biosweep Canada uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for legitimate business, operational, recordkeeping, and legal purposes. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and applicable requirements.

Authorized users may contact Biosweep Canada to request access to or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping obligations. Calendar events may also be retained in Google Calendar according to company policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for authorized business users and is not directed toward children under 13.
