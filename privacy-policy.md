# Privacy Policy for Biosweep Connect

**Effective Date:** September 19, 2026

Biosweep Connect ("the App") is operated by Biosweep. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App may collect:

- Email address used for account authentication.
- Receipt images uploaded through the App.
- Receipt information extracted from uploaded images, including vendor, date, total, and tax.
- Scoping information, including job notes, measurements, and cubic footage calculations.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide access to the App.
- Process and organize receipt information.
- Calculate and record scoping measurements.
- Send receipt and scoping reports by email.
- Maintain and improve the App.

## 3. Data Storage

The App uses Firebase services for authentication and data-related functionality. Information may be processed and stored by these services in accordance with their privacy policies.

## 4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the App or when required by law.

## 5. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained as needed to provide business services and fulfill legitimate business purposes. You may contact us to request deletion of your information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
