Privacy Policy for Biosweep Connect

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Biosweep Connect ("the App") is operated by Biosweep. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address used for account authentication.
  • Receipt images uploaded through the App.
  • Receipt information, including vendor, date, total, and tax.
  • Scoping information, including job notes and measurements.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide access to the App.
  • Process receipt information.
  • Calculate and record scoping measurements.
  • Send receipt and scoping reports by email.

3. Data Storage

The App uses Firebase services for authentication and data-related functionality. Information may be processed and stored by these services.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the App or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained as needed to provide business services. You may contact us to request deletion of your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
